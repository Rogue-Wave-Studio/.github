# Mission

Rogue Wave Studio is **design and technology for collective liberation.**

We design websites, apps, and interactive experiences for social justice
movements and art projects.

We exist to put high craft in the hands of people who are usually priced out of
it — organizers, movements, and artists doing urgent work with no access to the
caliber of design that corporations take for granted. We close that gap.

A rogue wave is sudden, disproportionate, and impossible to ignore. That's the
work we make: disproportionate to its budget, impossible to look away from, on
the side of the people who need it most.

## What we make

- **Websites** that give movements and artists a serious, credible presence.
- **Apps** that are useful, fast, and respect the people who use them.
- **Interactive experiences** that make a cause or a body of work *felt*, not
  just read.
- **Self-serve resources** we build proactively — open tools and templates,
  guides and toolkits, and public-interest data and interactive tools — so
  people can act on their own, without hiring us. We don't only wait to be
  commissioned; we arm people.

## Who we make it for

Social justice movements and art projects — collaborators in the trenches, not
clients at arm's length. We bring craft they'd typically never have access to,
and we bring it as partners in the work.
